About

I trained in Pharmacology at the University of Lagos (UNILAG / LUTH-MEDILAG) and later completed an M.Sc. in Public Health at the National Open University of Nigeria. Over the years I have worked across immunization campaigns, HIV/AIDS and TB support programmes, and community health outreach in Abuja and Benue State.

My research includes a published paper on the antidiarrhoeal effects of Sanseviera liberica root extract, and a recent dissertation examining factors that affect immunization programme implementation in Usafa Community, FCT.

In 2026 I completed certification as an AI Data Annotation Specialist. I bring the same attention to detail and evidence-based approach from clinical and public health work into structured data labeling, quality review, and evaluation tasks — particularly in health and life sciences domains.
